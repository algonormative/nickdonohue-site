# Interview Notes — 2026-03-09

## Core thesis (user's words)

"This cannot diminish the value of 'true' art, nor does this diminish the value of human effort. There will always be things for humans to do. We should be spending our efforts figuring out how to make the most of it and guiding our energy towards that goal."

## The gap in the discourse

- Pro-AI arguments focus purely on productivity — but nobody wants to wade through endless slop
- Anti-AI arguments focus on threat to craftsmanship and livelihoods — but ignore what's possible
- **The missing take:** Instead of wringing our hands, focus on creating wonderful things. The same tools that put us here will also pull us out.

## Key tensions to develop

1. **Productivity vs. quality** — Pro-AI crowd celebrates speed but doesn't address the slop problem. "Nobody wants to wade through an endless stream of slop."

2. **Empathy in both directions** — "I empathize with those who feel threatened and want to show them a better path. I empathize with those who see only opportunity and want to show them how to build higher quality products."

3. **Discourse incentives** — "Many of the incentives with online discourse seem to keep us locked into the same back-and-forth arguments." The platform dynamics reward extreme positions.

4. **Tools will solve what tools created** — "Naturally, the same tools that put us here will also pull us out." This is a strong claim worth developing.
